E-Tech hybrid simulator

An interactive, unofficial model of how Renault's full hybrid E-Tech combines a combustion engine, two electric motors and a clutchless dog-clutch gearbox. You drive with the accelerator, brake and regen paddles. The energy manager decides the mode and gear, and the HSG matches the engine's shaft speed before each dog clutch drops in.

Things to try

  • Gentle throttle (20–30%): the car stays electric up to roughly 70–80 km/h.
  • Floor it from a standstill: it launches on the e-motor, then around 20 km/h the HSG cranks the engine, matches it to gear 1 and the dog clutch engages. Every upshift repeats the sync.
  • Cruise above 80 km/h on light throttle: the engine drives and the HSG recharges the battery.
  • Drain the battery in EV: below about 28% the software forces series hybrid at low speed and engine + recharge above it, until the battery is back over 40%.
  • Lift off at different paddle levels: 0 coasts with everything decoupled, 1 to 3 give stronger regen. The brake uses regen first and the friction brakes cover the rest.

What's simplified

The decision rules approximate how the E-Tech energy manager behaves. They aren't Renault's calibration. Gear ratios, rpm values, battery rates and paddle strengths are illustrative, and the HSG's overshoot during sync is exaggerated so you can see it. The real dogbox has more dog clutches and gear pairs than the one sleeve per shaft drawn here, and the e-motor's own two-speed shift isn't animated in detail.
